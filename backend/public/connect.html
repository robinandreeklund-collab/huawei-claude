How to get a token
  1. On your computer: npm i -g @anthropic-ai/claude-code (if you don't have it already).
  2. Run claude setup-token and sign in with your Claude account in the browser.
  3. Copy the sk-ant-oat01-… token that's printed and paste it above.
  4. Press Connect — it's stored in the backend and used by the watch. Valid ~1 year.
The token is sent encrypted (HTTPS) and stored only on your server — never in the app or in git.
