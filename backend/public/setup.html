Connect your watch

Create an account and paste your own Anthropic API key. Everything Claude Code does from your watch runs on your Anthropic account and is billed to you — we never see your key beyond storing it for your sessions.

Get one at console.anthropic.com → API keys. It starts with sk-ant-api. This is the developer/API side of your Anthropic account (pay-as-you-go) — separate from a Max subscription.
